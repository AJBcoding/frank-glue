Optional texts to keep your software support request moving.

1. The program

Anthony Byrnes operates Frank Glue, a software support service for AI-coded applications and AI agent orchestrators. Clients who request support and opt in can receive one-to-one SMS about support appointments and schedule changes, troubleshooting questions and replies, and progress or resolution updates for active support requests. This SMS program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. Your agreement

Before sending texts, Anthony Byrnes provides and collects a paper SMS consent form in person. You enter your mobile number, check the initially unchecked consent box, and sign and date the form if you agree to receive support messages. Before you agree, the form provides the Frank Glue program name, the support messaging purpose, frequency and rate disclosures, STOP and HELP instructions, and links to these terms and our Privacy Policy.

You confirm that you want recurring, one-to-one software-support SMS from Frank Glue at the number you provide. Your agreement covers that purpose only. SMS participation is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing or receiving support. You can arrange support by email without enrolling in texts. Reading this website or emailing a request does not subscribe you. Anthony retains your signed, dated form as proof of permission and sends an enrollment confirmation before support texts begin. Paper forms are the only enrollment method; there is no advertised text-to-join keyword. You can read the blank paper form (PDF) before deciding.

3. Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with your support needs; messages may recur and there is no fixed messaging schedule. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile carrier plan. Contact your carrier for information about your plan.

4. Stopping messages

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.

You may receive a final message confirming your opt-out. We will not resume messaging unless you give new, valid permission.

You can also request that messages stop by emailing sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com. If you change or give up your phone number, please let us know so we can update our records.

5. Getting help

Reply HELP for assistance, or email sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com with a question about the program or your messaging choices.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on carrier networks and other factors. Frank Glue is not an emergency service.

Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information, including our commitment not to share mobile information or consent with third parties for their marketing.
