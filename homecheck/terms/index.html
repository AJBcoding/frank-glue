Anthony Byrnes operates HomeCheck as a privately configured iPhone app. Access is limited to people authorized by the operator. By using its messaging features, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, stop using those features.

2. Mail and messaging actions

HomeCheck provides access to configured Twilio conversations and a Gmail mailbox. Actions you take can send real messages, mark mail read, or move mail to Trash. You can stop using HomeCheck at any time. Revoke the notification authorization in your Google Account connections; revoke the separate Gmail app password to disable the mail connection as well. Contact the operator to remove retained credentials or installation records.
