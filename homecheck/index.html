HomeCheck

Your mail and text conversations, with an unread count on your Home Screen.

Mail and messaging

HomeCheck is a privately configured iPhone app operated by Anthony Byrnes. Its messaging features bring Twilio text conversations and a connected Gmail mailbox into the app.

The mail view supports Inbox and Sent, reading messages, composing and replying, and moving messages to Trash. These actions use the mailbox connection configured by the owner.

Gmail unread badges

When enabled, Gmail’s mailbox-change notifications let HomeCheck refresh its icon badge with the unread Gmail Inbox count plus unread texts. Delivery depends on Google, the backend, Apple, and the device; it is not guaranteed to be immediate.

The optional Google authorization for badges requests Gmail metadata access. This connection verifies the mailbox and checks its unread count. The badge push sent to Apple contains a number, without email subjects, senders, or message bodies.

Access and support

HomeCheck is configured for private use. This website explains the app; it does not provide public account registration.

Read the HomeCheck Privacy Policy for the separate mail and notification connections, data handling, and ways to disconnect access.
