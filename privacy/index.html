Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Personal conversations deserve clear expectations about personal information.

1. About this policy

Anthony Byrnes operates Frank Glue, a personal messaging project for voluntary, one-to-one SMS conversations with friends and family. This policy covers the Frank Glue messaging program and this website. For privacy questions, contact sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com.

2. Information we handle

We process the mobile number you provide, messages and any attachments you send, and information you choose to include, such as your name. Messaging providers also process conversation identifiers, message times, and delivery information.

Our paper consent process records your name, mobile number, signature, and signing date. We retain the signed form as proof of your permission to receive texts and keep records of your messaging preferences, including opt-out requests. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information in that correspondence.

This website has no online signup form, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts added by Frank Glue. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as your IP address to serve and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices.

3. How we use information

We use information to deliver and respond to personal messages you have agreed to receive, provide assistance, resolve delivery problems, protect the service, and respect your messaging choices. Consent and opt-out records help us document permission and prevent unwanted messages.

Frank Glue does not use this program to send advertisements or bulk marketing messages.

4. Service providers and sharing

Your mobile information stays out of marketing lists.

We never sell or rent your mobile information or SMS consent. We do not disclose phone numbers, mobile information, or opt-in and consent records to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for their marketing or promotional activities.

Twilio and telecommunications carriers process phone numbers, message content, and delivery information to provide messaging. Our email and website providers process information needed to deliver correspondence and this website. These providers receive information as needed to operate those services; this does not authorize them to use your SMS consent for their own marketing.

We may disclose information if required by law, limited to that required purpose. Receiving Frank Glue messages does not give any other sender permission to contact you. Read more about Twilio’s privacy practices.

5. Retention and security

We retain information while needed for your conversations, service operation, and applicable recordkeeping obligations. Consent and opt-out records may be retained to document your choices and honor requests not to receive messages. Provider systems and backups may retain information according to their own processes. Opting out does not automatically delete earlier messages.

We use account authentication and restrict access to our messaging accounts. SMS is not end-to-end encrypted; messaging providers and carriers process its content. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information by text.

6. Your choices

Participation is voluntary. You give explicit permission by completing, signing, and dating a paper form provided and collected in person by Anthony Byrnes before personal, one-to-one texts begin. You receive an enrollment confirmation after consenting and before personal conversations. Paper consent is the only enrollment method; there is no advertised text-to-join keyword. Message frequency varies with your conversations. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to stop messages. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com.

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete information we hold about you by emailing that address. We may need to verify your request. Some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations or honor an opt-out; we will explain relevant limitations when responding.

7. Updates and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, email sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com. Read our SMS Terms and Conditions for messaging details.
