Optional SMS support

Updates about
your request.

Clients who request help with AI-coded applications or AI agent orchestrators can choose one-to-one support texts from Anthony Byrnes through Frank Glue. Messages cover support appointments and schedule changes, troubleshooting questions, and progress or resolution updates about an active support request. This SMS program does not include marketing or promotional messages.

Anthony provides and collects the paper SMS consent form in person. It explains the support messaging program, frequency and rates, STOP and HELP instructions, and our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms. To enroll, enter your mobile number, check the initially unchecked consent box, sign and date the form, and return it to Anthony.

Anthony retains the signed form as proof of permission and sends an enrollment confirmation before support texts begin. Paper consent is the only enrollment method; there is no advertised text-to-join keyword.

Download the blank consent form (PDF) or view the blank form (image).

Message frequency varies with your support needs; messages may recur. Message and data rates may apply. SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing or receiving support. You can arrange support by email. Visiting this website or emailing a request does not enroll you in SMS.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also reach us at sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com.
