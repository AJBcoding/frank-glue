Before the first text

Know what you’re
agreeing to.

Anthony Byrnes provides and collects the paper consent form in person. Before you agree, the form explains Frank Glue’s personal messaging program, message frequency and rates, STOP and HELP instructions, and our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms. If you choose to join, enter your mobile number, check the consent box, sign, and date the form.

Anthony retains the signed form as proof of your permission. You then receive an enrollment confirmation before personal conversations begin. Paper consent is the only way to join; there is no advertised text-to-join keyword.

Download the blank consent form (PDF) or view the blank form (image).

Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is voluntary, and visiting this website does not sign you up.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. You can also reach us at sculler-floaty-0u@icloud.com.
